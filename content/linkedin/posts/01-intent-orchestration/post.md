## LinkedIn

### Post

Lately I've noticed AI in UX posts that sound super smart and deep, but leave you with nothing tangible to develop as a meaningful practice.

Screenshots, "10x research" claims, no study design, no artefact, no undo when the agent gets it wrong.   
  
What helped was not a cleverer prompt. Score outputs against agreed standards before anyone shares them - intent, pass bar, evidence attached. Iterations dropped where we enforced the checklist. First-pass acceptance went up.

**Takeaway:** score design outputs against standards before review - fewer iterations, higher first-pass acceptance.

First comment: full loop with Hermes rescoring design files; rubrics tighten when a row keeps failing.

Where does your team argue after the third revision?
