Toolkits

Reusable diagnostics from the work, packaged.

A toolkit is the structured way I run a specific kind of decision when a team brings it to me — not a Notion template or slide deck. The questions, the artefacts, and the handoff so your team can run it again.

How it works

Three steps from context to artefact

  1. Bring context. One decision, one constraint, one timeline — not a full product dump.
  2. Run the diagnostic. We work through the kit questions live (typically 60–90 minutes).
  3. Leave with the artefact. Written output your team can execute and revisit.

Scoped from Birlasoft and Fortune 100 field programmes — the same moves as the case-study method, packaged for a single decision.
