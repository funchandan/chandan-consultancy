Toolkits

Reusable diagnostics from the work, packaged.

A toolkit in this practice is a reusable diagnostic — the structured way I run a specific kind of decision when a team brings it to me. Not a Notion template, not a slide deck. The thinking, the questions, and the artifacts a team can run on their own once we've worked through it together.

Coming v1.1

Productized kits — coming v1.1

The kits I'm scoping cover three areas where teams keep arriving with the same shape of problem: decision teardowns (the bet, the assumptions, the kill criteria), architecture audits (where the seams are bending and why), and AI workflow design (where humans stay in the loop and where they shouldn't). Each kit captures the questions I ask, the artifacts I leave behind, and the runbook a team can re-execute without me in the room.

Until the v1.1 release ships them, the fastest way to use a toolkit is to bring the decision context to a teardown call. If you want a specific kit prioritized — or one I haven't named — say so:
