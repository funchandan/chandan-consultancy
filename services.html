Services

Embedded judgment for product, UX, and AI workflows.

I work with teams on roadmap clarity, design-system governance, and human-in-the-loop AI where mistakes are expensive. Engagements are scoped with clear artifacts and exit criteria — no open-ended retainers.

How it works

  1. Context. You bring a decision surface — a bet, a launch, or receipts that do not read clearly.
  2. Shape. I map the seams (people, systems, incentives) and name what has to be true for the next move to be safe.
  3. Ship + receipt. We pick a spine to ship first, then read what actually moved — wins, misses, and surprises.
