## Paige headings (source for HTML)

### 1. Context

Mass-market live learning across **eight countries** (APAC, North America). Organisation moved from **WhiteHat Jr** (2020–21) through **absorption into BYJU’s Future School (2021)** to portfolio-scale delivery. Sector footnote: edtech; **primary read:** consumer scale, channels, and reliability under growth pressure.

### 2. Segments and constraints

Household purchase dynamics, device and connectivity variance, support load, seasonal acquisition. Partnership surfaces (Disney, Roblox, Tynker) required **one research spine** feeding multiple roadmap threads.

### 3. Journey design

Manager, new initiatives (**M-WHJ-01**): 0→1 “Create with Math” — prototype, value testing, beta, GTM, NPS. Post-absorption: **Manager, user insights** — insights org, exec readouts, design system scale (**M-BYJ-04**–**07**).

### 4. Pilots

Structured research programme and shipped prototypes tied to engineering cycle reduction (**M-BYJ-07**); adoption on tracked releases (**M-BYJ-05**).

### 5. Impact signals

Category share (**M-BYJ-01**), MAU trajectory (**M-BYJ-02**), US subscription diagnostic (**M-BYJ-03**). **Paige rule:** “contributed to,” not solo revenue credit.
