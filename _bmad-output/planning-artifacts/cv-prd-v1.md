## 6. Content rules (party mode baseline + v1.1 critique)

### 6.1 Register (Paige — party critique)

- **CV body:** **Implied subject, verb-led bullets** (no “I,” no “Chandan is…”). This matches standard US résumé convention; **do not** switch to third-person bio voice (“Chandan manages…”).  
- **Portfolio / LinkedIn About:** May use **first person** where warmth helps (Mary: some Head-of-Design buyers want **human voice** outside the CV). Keep CV scannable and cold.

### 6.2 Arc titles — scannability (Paige)

- Titles must **map to job-description keywords** where possible: *Manager, new initiatives* and *Manager, user insights* are acceptable **if** each sub-role opens with **distinct outcome classes** (0→1 / GTM vs insights program, research ops, VP-facing readouts) so they do not read as **title inflation** after absorption (John).

### 6.3 Two “Manager,” lines — differentiation (John)

- **Mandatory:** First bullet under **WHJ** and first bullet under **BFS** must **not** share the same opening verb or sentence shape.  
- **WHJ chapter:** Own **0→1 / initiative / GTM / beta / NPS** — proof of ambiguous problem space.  
- **BFS chapter:** Own **insights org, volume, exec readouts, retention / subscription diagnostic, design system at scale, team of 8** — proof of operating maturity post-absorption.  
- **Absorption meta line:** Must not read passive (“ramping,” “learning”). Frame as **organisational fact + continuity of ownership**: e.g. continued through absorption; **mandate expanded** to portfolio-wide insights and delivery — only if true; adjust per PO.

### 6.4 Metrics — hedging and defensibility (Paige × John)

- **Policy:** Prefer **ranges, floors, ceilings**, or **named baselines** where Evidence Bank supports them. Avoid stacking “about” on every metric.  
- **Rule:** If a number cannot survive **one follow-up** (“from what baseline?”, “what was your lever?”), **drop the number** and keep a qualitative outcome or narrow the claim.  
- **Denominator / lever:** Where possible, bullets should imply **your decision unit** (not team vanity metric with unclear attribution).

### 6.5 Bullet density and AI-tells (Paige)

- **Theme-cluster:** Prefer **one crisp outcome per line** over micro-clause stacks; merge where it improves scan.  
- **Ban:** “Passionate,” ornamental em-dash sprawl, symmetrical synonym churn, dutiful restatement of the job description.  
- **Ban (prior list):** deck-speak (“spearheaded”, “10X”), ALL-CAPS section headers, unanchored “Led” without object.

### 6.6 Portfolio and links (Paige)

- **No** “update later” or dead portfolio line on outbound PDF — **live HTTPS URL** or **omit** until live. **v1.1 shipped with omit** on all `cv-*.html` contact blocks.

### 6.7 Voice for UX leadership buyers (Mary)

- CV stays dense; **design-variant summary** may carry **one half-line** of craft philosophy **without** marketing taglines — optional v1.1 copy pass (Sally + Paige gate).

### 6.8 Visual / brand (unchanged baseline)

- One accent use (hairline under name); Fraunces + Inter; `break-inside: avoid` on role blocks and on **WHJ→BFS group** (`.cv-role-group`).

## 7. Layout (Sally lock + party critique)

### 7.1 Baseline

- One page per variant; portfolio is trailer.  
- Asymmetric **35% / 65%**, **32px** gutter, 12-col mental grid, **~794px** A4 print width.  
- Print-first: page-break rules non-negotiable; **WHJ→BFS** stays one `.cv-role-group` unit.

### 7.2 Taste vs template (Sally — party critique)

- Current direction: **polished editorial template executed well** — acceptable for PM and enterprise scans.  
- For **Head of Design** buyers: add **one memorable narrative spine** (copy + one optional micro-visual on portfolio, not clutter on PDF): e.g. single line on **judgment in regulated + AI-assisted** contexts — not more decoration on the CV.  
- **“Design leadership taste”** is proven off-CV (site, case PDF) more than on one-page résumé; CV stays **quiet premium**, not gimmick.

### 7.3 Print and device QA (Sally)

- **Accent hairline:** stress-test on **laser print** and **grayscale export** — hairlines can vanish or band; if fail, thicken to 2px or rely on spacing alone.  
- **Sub-role indent:** verify **same wrap behavior** in Chrome print preview and headless PDF as on screen; broken wraps in narrow column = noise.  
- **Type pressure:** one-page forces small type — check **minimum readable size** after any bullet growth; prefer **cutting copy** over shrinking font.  
- **Orphans / widows:** spot-check first line of bullets and last line of sub-roles across variants.

### 7.4 Third-person + metrics “corporate PM” read (Mary)

- Acceptable for PM and many enterprise UX leaders. Mitigate for design-heavy buyers via **design variant summary + portfolio** (§6.7, §7.2).
