Decision teardown

When the team is debating options but nobody has written the bet. This kit forces one call, one kill criterion, and one reversal cost — in a single working session.

Who it’s for

Founders and PMs with one high-stakes decision in the next 4–8 weeks — pricing, build vs. buy, channel, or launch scope.

What you get

  • One-page bet memo (the call in plain language)
  • Pre-committed kill criterion and date
  • Reversal cost estimate (eng-weeks, not vibes)
  • Append-only decision log entry for the team

Sample output

Full redacted samples ship with v1.1. On a teardown call you’ll see the template and a walkthrough from the HP field service case.
