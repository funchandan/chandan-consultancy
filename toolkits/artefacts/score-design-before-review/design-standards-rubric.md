# Design standards rubric (starter template)

**Version:** 1.0 - customize for your team  
**Use:** Score design outputs **before** critique, stakeholder review, or handoff to build.  
**Pass threshold:** 14 / 16 (8 rows x 0-2)

Score each row **0-2**:

| # | Dimension | What "2" looks like |
|---|-----------|---------------------|
| 1 | **Intent captured** | User job, success metric, scope boundary |
| 2 | **Acceptance criteria** | At least 3 pass/fail checks (not "looks good") |
| 3 | **Design system fit** | Tokens, components, or patterns named |
| 4 | **Evidence artefact** | Wireframe, Figma frame, or spec link attached |
| 5 | **Accessibility baseline** | Focus order, labels, contrast, or WCAG target stated |
| 6 | **Edge states** | Empty, error, loading, or permission denied covered |
| 7 | **Undo / revision path** | How to revert or iterate if the output is wrong |
| 8 | **Review readiness** | Reviewer knows what to approve without a live walkthrough |

## How to use

1. Save this file where your team agrees (repo, wiki, or shared drive).
2. Fill the checklist when starting a design task or agent session.
3. Score the output **before** Slack, Figma comments, or review meetings.
4. If below threshold, fix intent or evidence - not another vague prompt tweak.

## Adapt for your team

Change rows 3 and 5 to match your design system and accessibility bar. Add a row only if the whole team will score it every time.

## When you score below 14/16

- **Any row at 0:** Stop. Missing artefact or intent, not "needs polish."
- **Two rows at 1 can still pass** at 14/16. Prioritize rows 1, 2, 4, and 6 first.
- **13 or below:** Do not share for review. Fix scope or edge states before re-prompting.
