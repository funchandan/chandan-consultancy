# Rubric design workshop (45 min)

**Goal:** Write scoring standards your team owns for **research** and **design** deliverables. Templates are starting points only.

**Who should join:** Someone who signs off research, someone who signs off design, and one person who will run the review loop (PM, ops, or engineer).

## 1. Name what you score (10 min)

List deliverables that get reviewed before they go to stakeholders:

| Output type | Where it lives (link or path) | Who approves |
|-------------|-------------------------------|--------------|
| Research synthesis | e.g. `work/research/checkout-study/synthesis.md` | Research lead |
| Interview guide | e.g. same folder, `guide.md` | Moderator |
| User flow / wireframe | e.g. Figma frame URL or `.excalidraw` file | Design lead |
| UI spec or prototype notes | e.g. `work/design/checkout/spec.md` | PM or design lead |

**Exit:** No deliverable is "in someone's head" or only in slide decks.

## 2. Pick dimensions (20 min)

For each output type, choose **6-8 rows** scored 0, 1, or 2.

**Research - common dimensions (pick 6-8):**

- Study question is clear and bounded
- Method fits the question
- Sample and recruitment are explained
- Claims link back to evidence (quotes, clips, data)
- Output is synthesis (themes), not a raw summary
- Limitations and bias are stated
- Design or product team knows what to do next
- A reviewer can approve without a live meeting

**Design - common dimensions (pick 6-8):**

- User goal and success measure are stated
- Acceptance checks are pass/fail, not "looks good"
- Matches your design system or component library
- Evidence file or frame is attached
- Accessibility expectations are stated
- Error, empty, and loading cases are covered
- There is a clear way to revise or revert
- A reviewer can approve without a live meeting

**Rule:** If two people would argue for an hour on a row, the wording is too vague. Rewrite it.

## 3. Set the pass bar (10 min)

| Dimensions | Max score | Typical pass |
|------------|-----------|--------------|
| 8 rows x 0-2 | 16 | 14 |
| 10 rows x 0-2 | 20 | 17 |

Write the pass rule at the top of each rubric file. Change it only with a dated team note.

## 4. Dry run (5 min)

Score one real deliverable from the last month. If two scorers differ by more than 3 points total, fix the rubric wording before you roll it out.

## Checklist before you leave the room

- [ ] Research rubric dimensions agreed and written down
- [ ] Design rubric dimensions agreed and written down
- [ ] Pass thresholds recorded
- [ ] Folder or link convention filled in (see `path-convention.md`)
- [ ] One practice score logged in your score log
