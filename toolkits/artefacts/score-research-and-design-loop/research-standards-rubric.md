# Research output rubric (starter template)

**Version:** 1.0 - replace after your workshop  
**Pass threshold:** 14 / 16 (adjust if you use more or fewer rows)

Score each row **0-2** (0 = missing, 1 = partial, 2 = meets bar):

| # | Dimension | What "2" looks like |
|---|-----------|---------------------|
| 1 | **Study question** | One clear question; scope says what is out of bounds |
| 2 | **Method fit** | Method matches the question; not "we used AI" alone |
| 3 | **Sampling** | Who was included and excluded, and why |
| 4 | **Evidence traceability** | Themes tie to quotes, clips, or source links |
| 5 | **Synthesis quality** | Insight and patterns, not bullet dumps |
| 6 | **Bias / limitations** | Gaps and risks are named |
| 7 | **Actionability** | Product or design knows the next decision |
| 8 | **Review readiness** | Approver can say yes/no without a read-aloud |

## How to use

1. Run the rubric design workshop. Change row names to match your team.
2. Save the file where your team agreed (see `path-convention.md`).
3. Score **before** you share synthesis in Slack, Notion, or review meetings.
4. Log the result in your score log. Do not share if below threshold.

## When you score below the pass bar

- **Any row at 0:** Stop. Something essential is missing (question, sources, or file).
- **Rows 4-5 at 1:** You likely have summary, not synthesis. Add evidence per theme.
- **At or below 13/16:** Fix scope or sources before asking for design handoff.
