How do we help field technicians act faster with AI they can trust?

Technicians in pilot reviews paused between AI diagnosis and parts ordering. I mapped the diagnosis-to-parts journey, designed AI state and override patterns in Figma, and validated flows with field pilots. I handed off React Native specs so the next step on the truck stayed clear and defensible.

Product HP Field Service

HP field technicians diagnose devices on site, talk with customers, and order parts under SLA pressure. The mobile experience spans unstable connectivity, parts accuracy, and explainable AI suggestions on the truck.

Problem Commit latency on the truck

Technicians paused between AI-assisted diagnosis and parts ordering. They had ranked suggestions but no single next step they could explain to the customer or defend to dispatch before scan-and-confirm.

Approach One path they can trust

I mapped the diagnosis-to-parts journey and designed one next-best action with explicit alternates, plain-language rationale on every suggestion, and override without penalty. Diagnosis context carries into parts without re-entry, and live, partial, and offline states are first-class so technicians know what is safe to act on.

Outcome Diagnosis to parts in one thread

Technicians could state why a part was suggested and change it without losing context. That removed the hesitation we saw when AI output and parts flow lived in separate mental models.
