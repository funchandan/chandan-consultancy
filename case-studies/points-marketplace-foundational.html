What should a loyalty program look like for young learners?

After class 8, attendance and time-on-platform dropped while points piled up unused. Quant and qual research showed learners did not understand the points marketplace. We reframed earn-and-spend before scaling rewards UI.

Problem Engagement & retention

Attendance and time-on-platform were falling while points piled up with no clear redeem path. Product was ready to scale marketplace and avatar UI without a shared earn-and-spend story. We needed a retention strategy grounded in how young learners read rewards, not another layer of opaque currency mechanics.

Solution Personalized marketplace

Journey work foresaw friction early and cut it before build. Design-system component design and spec-based engineering handoffs trimmed roughly three weeks from implementation. We planned a staged rollout by feature: ledger clarity and onboarding first, then marketplace depth, with badges, avatars, and level-ups on a foundation teams could ship in order.

Result Launch impact

43% higher attendance in the weeks from the launch milestone. 27% increase in the percentage of students who completed the course.

Insights: User testing

What kids said matched the screens they pointed at.

Fifteen sessions—each theme tied to a screen they pointed at.

  • Dual currency (points + coins) hides earn and spend.
  • Points sit unused; redeem affordance missing.
  • Avatars chosen for aspiration, not currency logic.

Design lens

Help prevent mistakes

Intent to redeem existed; balance showed without a next step.

Themes mapped to wallet and grid frames in Figma.

Recommendations: Recommendations and implementation guide

One ledger, onboarding before the shop, aspiration-led avatars.

Clarify earn-and-spend before scaling marketplace depth.

  • One balance, visible spend path, plain copy.
  • Deploy onboarding: how to earn and spend.

Recognition rather than recall
