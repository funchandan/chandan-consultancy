What should a loyalty program look like for young learners?

Attendance and time on platform were falling while points piled up with nowhere to go. I framed the retention gap with product, built design-system flows in Figma, and ran moderated sessions with learners in the US and India. I turned that work into one earn-and-spend story engineering could ship before marketplace UI scaled.

Product Byju's FutureSchool

BYJU's FutureSchool runs live math classes for grades 1-8 in the US and India. The product org was scaling rewards, avatars, and a points marketplace to keep learners motivated between classes.

Details

K-8 web & mobile · math learning platform
Design lead · system and UX role
Product · CXO · Engineering stakeholders
8 weeks duration
Tech stack

Problem Dual currency broke the loop

Learners earned points and creator coins but could not name a spend action. The system showed two balances with no shared ledger and no visible path from class wins to redeem. That user friction broke the earn-to-spend loop before marketplace UI scaled.

Approach Map, design, test, spec

I mapped the earn-to-spend journey with product, designed wallet and marketplace flows on the design system in Figma, and ran 15 moderated sessions in the US and India. I tied every quote to the screen in view and reviewed the same components with engineering before we locked ship order.

Outcome Earn-to-spend legible

We shipped one ledger with plain labels and spent history, plus earn-and-spend onboarding before marketplace browse. Learners could read balance and next action without inventing conversion rules. Avatar and shop depth moved to a second stage on that foundation.

Recommendations: Recommendations and implementation guide

Decisions engineering could schedule.

Evidence

One ledger, onboarding before browse, heroes over currency jargon.

Recommendations were ordered for ship: ledger and onboarding first, then marketplace depth, tokens, UX writing, in-product comms, and a single earn, balance, redeem journey.

Implementation guide

  • Ledger: one spendable balance, spent history, visible next action on marketplace.
  • Onboarding: earn and spend modules at deploy, before shop exposure.

Recommendations

  • Unify the ledger: spendable points balance with plain language and spent history. Ship as one marketplace surface in product.
  • Ship onboarding at deployment: 'How to win more points' and 'Steps to spend your points' before marketplace exposure.
