Brand system

Color, contrast, and usage

This site uses a small token set in styles.css (:root). Pairs below are checked for WCAG 2.1 level AA: normal text at least 4.5:1, large text (at least 24px regular or 18.67px bold) at least 3:1, and focus indicators visible against adjacent colors (2.4.11 / 2.4.7).

Principles

Primary green anchors the brand; secondary slate adds depth for data-heavy or “enterprise” moments without competing with the main accent. Semantic reds and greens are reserved for status and documentation—not decoration.

  • Use --color-text-muted or --color-text-lead for body and supporting copy on white, not mid-gray below 4.5:1.
  • On dark panels, use --color-text-on-inverse-muted or --color-text-on-inverse-soft only (solid tokens, verified on --color-surface-inverse).
  • Reserve --color-brand-highlight for large type on dark, eyebrows on inverse, or decorative gradients—not for long body text on white.

Focus & motion

Interactive elements use :focus-visible with a 3px outline and 3px offset via the shared --focus-ring token (blue on light surfaces; light blue on dark cards). Press Tab on this page to preview. Reduced motion prefers static hovers and disables decorative motion where animations are used.

Do / Don’t

Quick checks when adding new sections or client work.

Do

Pair semantic tokens with their documented backgrounds. Prefer CSS variables over one-off hex values so contrast stays predictable.

Don’t

Do not place mint (#9ff3d5) body copy on white or light gray; contrast fails for normal text sizes.

Homepage case-study card spec

The homepage case row in index.html follows one content hierarchy so visitors can scan domain, role, and outcome signals in less than 10 seconds before choosing a case.

  • Required order: kicker (domain + type), title, challenge summary, two outcome signals, role/scope line, single CTA.
  • Title style: outcome-oriented and specific; avoid generic claims without decision context.
  • Outcome signals: exactly two bullets per card, each mapping to one measurable axis (quality, cost, trust, retention, speed).
  • Role line: include ownership and program scope to clarify strategic contribution.
  • CTA pattern: keep one verb family per card type; use Read case study for client narratives and Read pattern study only for labeled research cards.

PO acceptance checklist

Run this checklist before publishing any homepage or case-card update.

  • Brand and token language in UI copy matches actual token behavior in styles.css for light and dark themes.
  • Case cards retain the documented hierarchy and contain two outcome signals each.
  • Role and scope are visible on every card to establish ownership credibility.
  • Card CTA copy is consistent with card type and links to the intended destination.
  • Focus states, keyboard access, and contrast remain compliant with WCAG 2.1 AA expectations.
  • Claims are evidence-oriented (specific directional outcomes) and avoid unsupported superlatives.
