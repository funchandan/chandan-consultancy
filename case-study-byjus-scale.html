Secondary case · India-scale consumer

Scaled consumer journeys across eight markets

WhiteHat Jr through absorption into BYJU’s Future School: 0→1 launches, insights org leadership, and design-system scale where adoption, retention, and research volume had to move together.

🌏 Eight countries (APAC, North America)

📊 Manager, new initiatives → Manager, user insights

🧪 800+ hours structured research, 300+ users

📐 Design system: 30+ components, three squads

CTContext

Mass-market live learning under portfolio pressure.

The organisation moved from WhiteHat Jr (2020–21) through active absorption into BYJU’s Future School (2021) to portfolio-scale delivery across multiple brands and partnership surfaces (Disney, Roblox, Tynker).

Sector footnote: Edtech is named for honesty; the Deloitte read is multi-market delivery, channels, device variance, and reliability at scale—not a consumer-only brand pitch.

SGSegments and constraints

Household purchase, connectivity, and support load.

Household purchase dynamics, device and connectivity variance, seasonal acquisition, and support load shaped what “good” journeys had to prove. One research spine fed multiple roadmap threads so exec readouts stayed comparable quarter to quarter.

JRJourney design

0→1 launch, then insights org at scale.

Manager, new initiatives: “Create with Math”—prototype, value testing, beta, go-to-market, and NPS instrumentation.

Manager, user insights (post-absorption): Insights organisation, exec readouts, and design-system scale across squads—handoff velocity and consistency gains tracked against engineering cycle time on scoped releases.

PLPilots and adoption

Shipped prototypes tied to engineering cadence.

Structured research volume and shipped prototypes aligned with measured cycle-time reduction on scoped releases; tracked releases reported strong adoption where instrumentation existed.

IMImpact signals

Metrics are programme-level; credit is contributory.

Illustrative signals aligned to the CV and internal manifest: category share of business revenue for math, MAU growth across eight countries, US subscription and refund diagnostics, research hours and user counts, design-system coverage, and adoption on tracked releases.

Limits: Client metrics and internal targets are partially redacted; public numerics stay in sync with the same release tag as the CV HTML/PDF.
