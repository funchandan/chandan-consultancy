Case Study 02

HP: AI-Assisted UX for Field Service Operations

Designing AI support workflows that help field agents diagnose faster, act with confidence, and reduce operational turnaround time.

Focus AI UX, field operations, decision support
Primary goal Reduce service turnaround time
Role UX strategy, workflow design, service systems

Context

At Birlasoft, I worked on HP's field service ecosystem, where operational agents managed on-site diagnostics, customer interactions, and parts workflows. The experience had to support fast decisions in dynamic, information-heavy environments.

Problem

Field agents often faced delayed diagnosis, fragmented information, and manual parts-ordering steps. These frictions increased turnaround time, created avoidable repeat visits, and reduced confidence in first-time resolution.

Constraints

The system had to perform in real-world service conditions with variable data quality, inconsistent network environments, and tight operational SLAs. It also needed to preserve agent judgment while introducing AI guidance.

Approach

I mapped end-to-end service journeys, identified high-friction decision points, and designed AI-assisted interaction flows for diagnosis, recommendation, and ordering pathways. The UX principle was clear: assistance should be inspectable, contextual, and actionable in the moment of need.

Key decisions

  • Prioritized "next best action" guidance over generic recommendation lists.
  • Designed part-identification support around scan-plus-confirm patterns to reduce errors.
  • Made confidence cues and rationale visible so agents could trust or override suggestions.
  • Aligned diagnostic and ordering flows to reduce handoff and re-entry friction.

Solution

The resulting concept introduced an AI-assisted service flow where agents could scan components, receive likely issue diagnosis, and trigger parts recommendations with clearer context. The workflow was designed to reduce cognitive load while preserving field-level control.

Outcomes

The design direction improved clarity and speed across service workflows, supporting lower turnaround time, stronger first-time resolution behavior, and better operational consistency across agent experience levels.

What I learned

In AI-assisted operations, adoption depends less on "smartness" alone and more on whether the system strengthens frontline agency. The best AI UX turns uncertainty into confident action without removing human ownership.
